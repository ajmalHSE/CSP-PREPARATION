Source preservation

All 2,698 completely extracted items were compared with their original PDF text: questions, every option, source answer labels and full explanations. Content in the database matches the extracted source strings. PDF line wrapping and paragraph whitespace were consolidated during extraction; wording was not corrected or rewritten. The 14 incomplete records retain the available raw source fragment. Answers are source answers, not independently verified corrections.

Download exclusions and reasons. Each playable question links to its source PDF page after you answer.

Validation

250 consecutive generated games passed the 50-question quota, exact 15/35 source split, uniqueness and domain-order checks. All 2,219 eligible questions were covered across 111 occupied page ranges. Correct-question repeats occurred only after their domain/source pool had exhausted unseen questions. Game-1 wrong questions returned as 4, 4 and 2 in games 5, 6 and 7, with a fresh delay if missed again. Scoring, answer locking, resume, bookmarks, domain practice, wrong-answer practice and end-of-game review passed automated engine and UI-control tests. Offline shell, question data, report and opened PDF caching passed a service-worker harness.

Phone viewport styles and large touch targets are implemented. Real browser visual layout, home-screen installation and device offline behavior still require a phone/browser check: the available browser could not connect to the local preview. Automated harness tests do not replace those device checks.
