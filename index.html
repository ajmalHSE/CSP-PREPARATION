How practice works

Each random game has 50 unique questions. Domains 1 and 2 split their 25 questions as 12/13 or 13/12. Domains 3–7 have five each. Questions are mixed so consecutive questions come from different domains.

Study materials have priority, with about 75% of each domain selection taken from study questions where available. Calculations and formula items are excluded.

Wrong-answer and bookmark practice use up to 50 saved questions. If fewer are available, the session is shorter. Domain practice has 50 questions from the selected domain.

Answers and explanations follow your PDFs exactly; they have not been independently corrected. Incomplete items are flagged and held out. Your game, wrong answers and bookmarks are saved on this device.
